Personal care · BookingAug 2023 → present937 commits

Dorey

A queuing app that gets people into the chair on time instead of into a waiting room.

Customers find a barbershop or salon, pick a service and a barber, and take a real slot that nobody else can take. Shops run their whole day from a separate business app: branches, staff schedules, the check-in desk, promotions and reports. Built generic enough to take on other service verticals later.

Customer app

Browse, book, show up
  • Quick search across shops, branches and services (fuzzy, trigram-indexed)
  • Slot picker that updates live as other people book, for one barber or "any barber"
  • Hold → confirm booking flow: the slot is held for 5 minutes while the customer finishes, with deposits wired in
  • Phone sign-in with OTP, bookmarks, booking history, reviews
  • Reminder an hour before, cancellation, and notices when the shop changes a booking
  • Arabic and English, including notification text

Business app

Owners, branch managers, receptionists, admins
  • Branches, barbers, services, prices and weekly schedules with breaks
  • Check-in desk and no-show handling, with a 24-hour undo
  • Editing a schedule previews which bookings it would cancel before saving
  • Promo codes per branch, shop clients, staff accounts with approval flow
  • Dashboards and deep analytics: bookings, revenue and finance over any date range
  • Bulk import of barbers and services from spreadsheets

Backend

Supabase · 84 migrations · 5 Edge Functions
  • Postgres with row-level security on every table; roles minted into the login token by a database hook
  • Booking server functions: hold_booking, confirm_booking, create_booking
  • Push notifications through a queue with retries and a dead-letter list
  • Edge Functions for OTP, push, geocoding, storage clean-up
  • 37 database test suites, run in CI
Problems solved

No double bookings

Two customers can never get the same barber at the same time, even if they tap at the very same second. The second person is told straight away that the time is taken.

No duplicate bookings or charges

If a customer's connection drops while booking and they try again, they get the booking they already made, never a second one.

Staff can't overwrite each other

If two managers edit the same shop at once, the second is warned instead of silently erasing the first person's changes.

Safe schedule changes

Before a shop changes its hours, the app shows exactly which bookings would be affected, and those customers are notified.

Availability that is always current

Open times update on screen as other people book, so customers don't choose a time that has already gone. It stays fast and affordable even when a busy shop has many people looking.

Fixes without store delays

A new feature can go to a few customers first, or be switched off within minutes, with no app store review. Errors are reported automatically, so issues are fixed before customers complain.

Egypt's running scene in one app, and the race-day console that runs the events.

Runners discover clubs and events on a map, register, track runs with GPS and see their results. Club organisers and event staff use a second app to create runs, plan courses, check people in at the gate, manage sponsors and booths, and watch the event live.

Discover, register, run

  • 3D course fly-through of the race route; route builder snapped to real paths
  • Event registration forms, QR ticket, results and podium

Mgmt app

Club owners, event staff, admins

Problems solved

Runs are never lost

Tracking keeps going with the screen off or the phone in a pocket, and the run is saved as it happens, so a crash doesn't erase it.

Works with runners' watches

Runners link their Garmin or WHOOP once and their workouts appear on their own. Account details are kept securely on the server, never on the phone.

Quick check-in on race day

Staff scan bibs and tickets at the gate and at sponsor booths and get a clear yes or no on screen, built for crowds and weak mobile signal.

See the race route before race day

Runners can fly through the course in 3D, with the elevation, so they know what's coming.

Organisers see the race live

During an event, organisers follow runners on a live map from their phone.

Course records kept automatically

When a runner sets the best time on a club route, the record updates by itself, with nothing entered by hand.

Health · E-commerce · DeliveryFeb 2026 → in developmentCustomer + 4 staff roles

Sehety

An online pharmacy that remembers your medication and brings it to your door.

Customers search or scan a medicine, upload a prescription, keep a list of their regular medication and a vault of medical documents, and pay by wallet or cash. Admins, doctors, moderators and drivers each sign in to one management app and see only their own tools.

Customer app

Order, track, refill
  • Search with popular searches, category grid, filters by price, brand and symptom
  • Barcode scanning, request an unavailable medicine, upload a prescription
  • "My medications" list for one-tap refills; product bundles
  • Cart suggestions from reorder history and frequently-bought-together
  • Checkout with live stock check, wallet or cash, map pin for the address
  • Live order tracking, cancellation, loyalty points convertible to wallet
  • Medical document vault with folders, PDF and image previews

Mgmt app

Admin · doctor · moderator · driver
  • "Needs attention" dashboard, order pipeline with sound alerts for new orders
  • Catalog: medications, categories, manufacturers, suppliers, units, Excel import
  • Home-screen builder, ads, curated popular searches, bundles
  • Wallets with audited balance adjustments, promo codes, best sellers, reports
  • Driver shifts and a driver order view; doctor and moderator dashboards
  • Role management with a gate that sends each person to their own area

Backend

Supabase · 26 migrations · push function
  • Money, stock and role changes only through server functions, never direct writes
  • Row-level security per role; schema changes linted and checked by advisors
  • Push notifications through an Edge Function bridge to FCM
Problems solved

No surprises at checkout

Stock is checked just before an order is placed. If something has just sold out, the cart updates and the customer sees why, instead of the pharmacy calling them later.

One app for the whole team

Admins, doctors, moderators and drivers use one app, and each person sees only their own tools. Access is also enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

Every pound accounted for

Every wallet change, points conversion and manual adjustment is recorded, so the customer and the admin can always see where a balance came from.

Refills in one tap

Customers keep a list of their regular medication and reorder it without searching again.

Chicco

A coffee shop's whole operation, from the custom latte to the delivery door.

Customers build their own drinks from real ingredients, pay by card, Apple Pay or wallet, and collect points. Baristas work an order queue, drivers deliver and confirm with a code, and managers see revenue, stock and expenses, all in one app that changes with the signed-in role.

Client

Order and earn
  • Menu by category and size; custom blends from ingredients and toppings
  • Sign in with Google, Apple, Facebook or phone OTP
  • Card payments through Paymob, Apple Pay, wallet top-up
  • Points and rewards, favourites, reviews, QR codes
  • Live order tracking and delivery address on the map

Staff roles

Barista · delivery · manager · admin · partner
  • Barista order queue with preparation details
  • Delivery assignments, navigation and code-verified hand-off
  • Products, ingredients, topping stock, sizes, stores, cities and delivery fees
  • Promo codes, segmented push campaigns, reward programmes
  • Revenue analytics, recurring expenses and a daily analytics digest

Backend

Firebase · 18 Cloud Functions
  • Paymob webhook and wallet payment functions
  • Points awarded only after payment is confirmed server-side
  • Delivery OTP verification, role claims, promo redemption
  • Firestore security rules, App Check
Problems solved

Payments that can't be faked

An order is only marked paid once the payment company confirms it, so nobody can trick the app into a free order. Loyalty points are only given after payment.

Orders reach the right person

The driver can only complete a delivery with a code from the customer.

No ordering what's sold out

When an ingredient runs out, the drinks that need it show as unavailable before they reach the cart.

Six roles, one app

Customers, baristas, drivers, managers, admins and partners all use the same app. Each sees only what their role allows, and the server checks it too.

How a project runs

From first call to running in production

The order below is how every project above was built. You see working software early and every week after.

Step 1

Discover

Who uses it, who runs it, and where money or time is lost today. Written up as screens and roles.

Step 2

Model

Tables, permissions and the rules that must never break (no double booking, no negative stock).

Step 3

Build

Customer and staff apps grow together on one backend, with a test build to try every week.

Step 4

Harden

Retries, offline cases, security review, load on the busy paths, and automated tests.

Step 5

Launch & run

Store release, monitoring, force-update and feature flags, then ongoing support.
